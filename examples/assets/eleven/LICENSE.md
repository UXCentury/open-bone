# Example art, sounds and voices (this folder)

Everything in this folder was generated by UXCentury (the project owner) with ElevenLabs for the OpenBone examples:
images with gpt-image-2.5 in ElevenLabs Flows (backgrounds removed afterwards), sound effects with ElevenLabs Sound
Effects v2, and voices with ElevenLabs v4. UXCentury owns them and releases them under the MIT License, like the rest
of this repository, so you can reuse them in your own OpenBone projects.
