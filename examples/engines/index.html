OpenBone in web engines

Add ?example=campfire (or rocket, crash, button, walker...) to a page to play another example. The painted knight (examples/knight.obone) played by each engine runtime. Click the road to walk, the knight to attack, the shield to salute. Sound starts after the first click.

  • PixiJS v8 (@openbone/pixi)
  • Phaser 3 (@openbone/phaser)
  • Three.js (@openbone/three)
  • CanvasKit / Skia (@openbone/canvaskit)
