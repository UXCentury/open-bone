## Add an emitter

<Steps>

1. Select the slot that should emit (usually on a bone you can move, like the rocket's `nozzle`).
2. In the Inspector choose **+ Particles…** and pick a preset: fire, smoke, sparks, confetti, exhaust or coins. Or start
   empty.
3. Tune it in the **Particles** section:
   - **Emission**: emitting on or off, rate per second, a maximum, duration, loop, prewarm and **bursts** (a count at a
     time in each cycle);
   - **Shape**: point, circle, box or cone, and world or local space;
   - **Life**: lifetime, speed, gravity, drag;
   - **Over life**: size, rotation and colour curves;
   - **Look**: dots, squares, sparks or **any image** in the document as the particle.
4. **★ Save as preset…** keeps your settings as your own preset, available in every project.

</Steps>

Particles are **seeded**, so the editor's preview, Play mode, the runtimes and the video exports all show the same
particles frame for frame.

## Drive emitters

- **Keys**: `emitting` and `rate` are animatable properties (the ◆ next to them).
- **Listeners**: the *start particles* action bursts an emitter on a click.
- **States**: a state's animation keys the emitters it needs, so the state machine turns them on and off.

## Sprite sequences

For effects drawn frame by frame (flames, explosions, a blinking light), use a **sequence** attachment: **+ Add →
Sequence** takes numbered images (`fire_0001.png`…), a sprite sheet (a grid, TexturePacker or Aseprite JSON) or a ZIP
of frames. Each sequence has its own fps and mode (loop, once, ping-pong, hold last), and can be driven by time, by
keys or by a number input.
