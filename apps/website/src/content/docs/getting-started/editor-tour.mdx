## The layout

| Area | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Toolbar** (top) | Menu, undo/redo, **▶ Play**, the **Setup / Animate** switch, the tools, **Bone / Part**, the pick filter, **+ Add**, mute, **View**, shortcuts and **⚡ Connect MCP** |
| **Skeleton** (left) | The tree of bones, slots and attachments, with search, eyes (visibility) and locks; below it skins, images, audio, animations, events and state machines |
| **Viewport** (centre) | The artboard. Wheel zooms, <kbd>Alt</kbd>/right-drag pans, <kbd>F</kbd> fits. Grid, rulers and guides, snapping, background and onion skin are in its corner |
| **Inspector** (right) | Properties of whatever is selected: the document, a bone, slot, attachment, constraint, sound or bus. **Preview** shows the result as shipped, **Outline** lists everything |
| **Timeline** (bottom) | The dopesheet or graph of the current animation, the **State Machine** editor and the **Scripts** panel |

Every panel can be dragged to another side or grouped with others, and **☰ Menu → Layout** saves your own layouts.

## Setup and Animate

- **Setup mode** builds the rig: bones, slots, attachments, constraints and their rest values (the *setup pose*).
- **Animate mode** (<kbd>`</kbd> toggles) records keys. With **● Rec** on, any change you make at the playhead becomes a
  key in the current animation; the Inspector's ◆ buttons key one property.

## Tools

| Tool | Key | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Select / rotate | <kbd>V</kbd> | Click to select, drag to rotate bones (or parts, in **Part** mode) |
| Translate | <kbd>T</kbd> | Move bones and attachments |
| Scale | <kbd>S</kbd> | Scale; Shift for uniform |
| Pose (IK) | <kbd>I</kbd> | Drag a bone and its parents bend to follow |
| Create bone | <kbd>B</kbd> | Drag from a parent bone to add a child |
| Mesh | <kbd>M</kbd> | Edit mesh vertices; in Animate mode, free-form deformation |
| Weights | <kbd>W</kbd> | Paint how much each bone moves each vertex |
| Pen | <kbd>N</kbd> | Draw bezier paths and bounding-box outlines |
| Compensate | <kbd>J</kbd> | Move a bone without moving its children |

**Bone | Part** decides whether dragging a picture moves its bone or the picture itself. **Pick** filters what a click can
grab. A hover outline always shows what the next click will select.

## The menu

<Shot name="editor-menu" alt="The main menu: New project, Open, Save, Save as, Save to folder, Export, Import from project, Watch art for changes, Watch folder, Examples, Layout, Show panel, Keyboard shortcuts, About OpenBone" />

**Import from project…** copies animations, skins or a whole skeleton from another file. **Watch art for changes**
reloads images and sounds when you save them in your paint or audio app. **Layout** and **Show panel** manage the
dockable workspace.

## Play mode

**▶ Play** (<kbd>P</kbd>) runs the file as it will ship, with its state machine, scripts, sound and pointer
interaction. The **Inputs** panel sets inputs and fires triggers, and the log shows every state change. See
[State machines](/guides/state-machines/).
